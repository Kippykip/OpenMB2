/// INFO ///
Use your mouse to control the bat.

Hold the right mouse button down,
and then drag it to change the camera angle.
Use the mouse wheel to zoom in or out.

Press ESC to pause.
Press S to save game.
Press L to load game.
Press F6 to change music.
Press F7 to change background.
Press F8 to change board texture.
